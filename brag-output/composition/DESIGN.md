# FieldLink Video Design

## Style Prompt
A calm emergency-operations instrument built from graphite panels, warm paper-white type, one safety-lime route signal, and precise incident-log typography. The interface should feel field-ready and auditable, with quiet hairlines and map-like structure rather than futuristic AI effects.

## Colors
- `#101412` — canvas
- `#171C19` — raised instrument surface
- `#303732` — hairline and inactive route
- `#F2F4E9` — primary text
- `#A9B2AC` — secondary text
- `#D7FF61` — local route and primary status
- `#FF8E63` — danger qualifier only

## Typography
- `IBM Plex Sans` — titles, body, controls
- `IBM Plex Mono` — timestamps, sources, route metadata

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients, neon glow, glass, floating orbs, or generic AI imagery
- No pill-heavy interface or equal-width feature-card grid
- No fabricated metrics, medical claims, or unsupported features
- No uppercase tracking except compact operational metadata
- No looping ambient motion
