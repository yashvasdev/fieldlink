# Brag Plan: FieldLink

## What is this app?
FieldLink is an offline-first disaster guidance prototype that uses local models to select structured identifiers while stored, source-attributed guidance supplies the actions.

## The angle
Present FieldLink as an emergency instrument rather than an AI chatbot: a quiet, field-ready console that routes a stressful description through local inference and deterministic checks into vetted offline guidance. The redesigned screen is a presentation treatment for the video; every capability, scenario, guide title, and source is grounded in the working project.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“When the network disappears, guidance shouldn’t.” A thin local-route line connects three instrument nodes while the FieldLink wordmark resolves.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A real demo scenario is entered: earthquake, power outage, suspicious tap water, insulin needs, an older adult, and a pet.
- The screen explicitly shows `Local model ready`, `No cloud`, and the route `Input → Llama 3.2 → Stored guidance`.
- The verified result becomes three prioritized, source-attributed guide cards: earthquake safety, unsafe water, and medication continuity.
- The boundary line appears verbatim: “AI prioritized these guides. It did not write the safety steps.”

## Outro / punchline
“Designed for infrastructure failure.” FieldLink lands beside `Local inference · Stored guidance · Zero cloud calls` and `HackMIT 2026`.

## User flow worth showing
Describe a disaster and household needs → local model ranks supported guide IDs → stored guide cards appear in priority order.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm emergency-operations product film
- Interpretation: restrained motion, instrument-like typography, quiet graphite surfaces, one safety-lime accent, and confident holds instead of frenetic AI effects.

## Duration: 20 seconds

- Music cue guidance: 109.96 BPM preset; use 8.74s for the action commit, 13.11s for the plan reveal, and 17.47s for the outro transition; guide-card labels may align to 13.11s, 14.20s, and 15.29s with adequate hold time
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; deliberate restraint suits the emergency-instrument direction
- SFX posture: sparse; one low-risk click, one soft reveal impact, and one restrained logo bell

- Restraint rule: no cinematic booms, alarms, glitch effects, or typing noise under readable text
